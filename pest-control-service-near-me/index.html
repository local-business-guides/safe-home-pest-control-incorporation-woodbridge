Prepare for your nearby pest control visit

You don't need to rearrange the entire house before speaking with us. Start with a simple record of the problem, then follow the instructions provided for your visit.

  • Note which rooms or outdoor spots have activity.
  • Record when you first noticed the issue and when you tend to see it.
  • List any sprays, powders, traps or other products you've used.
  • Keep product packaging or photos of labels available.
  • Arrange access to the affected areas without disturbing suspected nests.
  • Ask how pets, food and personal belongings should be handled.

If you're renting, check with your landlord or property manager about arranging access and authorizing work. In a shared building, explain if the problem appears to involve more than your own unit. That information helps frame the inspection.

Avoid mistakes when hiring pest control nearby

Repeatedly applying different products can make the situation harder to assess. Before adding another treatment, tell the technician what you've already used and where. Never mix pest control products or use them in ways their labels don't allow.

Another mistake is expecting a quote without providing enough detail. Describe the property, the affected spaces and the signs you've noticed. Ask what remains uncertain until an inspection takes place, rather than treating an early estimate as a complete diagnosis.
